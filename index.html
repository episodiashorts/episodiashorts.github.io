Every season, a new world.

EpisodiaShorts is a channel of short animated stories, on YouTube and Instagram. A new episode comes out every day, and every season is set in a brand-new world with its own characters.

EpisodiaShorts Studio

EpisodiaShorts Studio is the channel's own production tool. It runs on the channel owner's computer and helps make each episode with AI-assisted writing, art, animation and narration. The owner reviews and approves every script and every finished video before it goes out.

Once a video is approved, the Studio uploads it to the EpisodiaShorts YouTube channel and posts it to the EpisodiaShorts Instagram account. It signs in only to the channel's own accounts and is not offered to anyone else.

Read how the Studio handles account data in the Privacy Policy.
